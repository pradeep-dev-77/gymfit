Gym Rules

  1. Carry your membership card or ID for every visit — front desk cannot admit you without it.
  2. Re-rack weights and wipe down equipment after use.
  3. Closed-toe athletic shoes required on the training floor at all times.
  4. Book squat racks and platforms for a maximum of 45 minutes during peak hours (5–8 AM, 6–9 PM).
  5. No dropping loaded barbells; controlled lowering only.
  6. Personal training sessions must be scheduled at least 12 hours in advance through the front desk or your trainer.
  7. Guests may enter with a member for a one-time trial visit only.
  8. Phones on silent; keep calls to the lounge area, not the training floor.
  9. Members under 16 must be accompanied by a guardian at all times.
  10. Management reserves the right to suspend membership for repeated rule violations.
